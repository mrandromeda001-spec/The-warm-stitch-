Our Story

A hook, some wool, and a lot of patience

I picked up a crochet hook a little over a year ago, mostly out of curiosity. My mother used to knit sweaters for us every winter, and watching her hands move made me want to try it myself. My first attempt was a crooked little flower — it took me two evenings and I still have it.

Somewhere along the way, I started making poshak for Laddu Gopal for our own home mandir. Neighbours noticed, relatives asked for one too, and that's really how The WarmStitch began — from Pugal, one order at a time.

Every piece that leaves my hands is looped by hand, checked twice, and packed with the same care I'd want for my own Thakur ji. Nothing is mass-made here.

  • 100% handcrafted, no machine work
  • Made-to-size Laddu Gopal poshak
  • Custom colours on request
  • Direct from maker, no middlemen
  • Local delivery across Bikaner
